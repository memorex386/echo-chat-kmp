Tap any photo to open it full screen. State IDs match docs/spec.md. Everything outside the phone is a review tool, not part of the app.

      The typing dots and GIFs are the only loops, and the dots stop as soon as the last reply lands. Nothing is hidden at rest. Compose follows the system animation scale, so "remove animations" turns all of this off.

      For review

      1. One typing indicator under the newest message, from send until the last waiting reply lands.
      2. Echo's avatar is a warm orange “E” on every reply and on the dots. Google Messages shows it only on the last of a run, but here replies rarely run together.
      3. + opens an attach panel: Gallery, Camera and an optional GIF. In the app, Gallery opens Android's photo picker and Camera opens the camera app.
      4. Photo echo: the same photo comes back, captioned “You said: …” or “You sent a photo”.
      5. Carried over from round one: the empty-state hint, “You said: Hello”, messages gathered at the bottom, and the fixed teal palette.
